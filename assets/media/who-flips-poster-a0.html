Persuasive, yet hard to flip.

07 / STRONGER CHALLENGES

MaxFlip amplifies flips.

Selecting across source models raises AFR
by up to 23.6 percentage points.

Versus same-model blind challenges.
No clear increase for GPT-5.1.

08 / HOW MAXFLIP WORKS

Pool. Test. Select.

Pool wrong-answer arguments across sources;
keep the most effective one for each question.

Schematic. Selection and evaluation use the same
model set; transfer to held-out models is untested.

Evaluate stability
alongside accuracy.
